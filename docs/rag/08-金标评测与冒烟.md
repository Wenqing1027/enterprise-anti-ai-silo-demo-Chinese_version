## 1. 门槛

| 指标 | 门槛 | 含义 |
|------|------|------|
| `run_ok_rate` | 100% | 控制环成功返回 |
| `hit_doc_recall` | ≥80% | expect_hit 用例：引用命中金标 `doc_id_any_of` |
| `cite_present_rate` | 100% | expect_hit 用例必须有 citations |
| `keyword_hit_rate` | ≥70% | 答案含 `must_contain_any` 之一 |
| `domain_isolation_rate` | 100% | 引用域 ⊆ Skill `kb_domains_allow` |
| `cross_domain_safe_rate` | 100% | 越域：不泄漏禁文档 + 答案承认未覆盖/引导 |
